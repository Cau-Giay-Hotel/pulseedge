FAQ
Do I need to create an account?

No. PulseEdge has no sign-up, no login, and no server-side account of any kind — everything runs and is stored on your device.

Is PulseEdge free?

Yes, free to download and use, supported by ads. Optional weekly, monthly, or lifetime Premium plans are available if you'd like to support development — no feature in this version is locked behind them.

Does it work on phones without a hardware Always-On-Display?

Yes. PulseEdge's AOD screen is drawn by the app itself on your lock screen, so it works even on phones that don't have a hardware AOD.

Does PulseEdge record my audio?

No. It only reads the volume/amplitude of audio already playing on your device to drive the animation — nothing is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Denying microphone access just switches the effect to a random pattern.

Which permissions does it need, and why?

Display over other apps (to draw the edge effect), notification access (to show the playing track and a notification dot on AOD), and optionally microphone (to react to audio). Usage access is requested separately if you use the per-app selector.
