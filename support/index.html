Before you write in, a few quick answers

  • The edge-lighting effect doesn't react to music — check that you granted the Microphone permission (Settings → Apps → PulseEdge → Permissions). Without it, the effect runs in Random Mode instead of reacting to audio.
  • The overlay/AOD doesn't show on top of other apps — make sure "Display over other apps" is enabled for PulseEdge in your device Settings.
  • Now-playing song info doesn't appear on the AOD screen — this requires Notification access to be granted to PulseEdge (Settings → Apps → Special app access → Notification access).
  • Managing or cancelling a Premium subscription — this is done through Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions, not inside the App. See our Refund Policy for purchase issues.
